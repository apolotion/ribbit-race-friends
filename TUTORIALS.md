# Tutorial Overview — Ribbit Race Friends 1.7.8

The first-boot Single Player tutorial starts only after account authentication or **Play Without Saving** and runs in the real Park engine.

## Presentation

- Ember speaks from a compact top panel with a right-side portrait and changes expression between lines.
- One consistently sized sentence types at a time; longer copy wraps to a second centered line.
- Hold Space for 2× typing speed, quick-tap Space/Enter to reveal or advance, or tap-and-release on mobile.
- A two-frame down triangle appears only when more dialogue remains.
- Park animation and authored fly movement continue behind dialogue while frog movement is locked.
- The first dinner fly poofs in before Ember announces it.
- Current Goal stays centered and provides a hover **HOW TO** explanation.
- Pause remains available with **Retry Scene** and **Skip Tutorial**.

## Lessons

1. Welcome and objective.
2. Three consecutive 90%-plus charged jumps, each confirmed by a landing sparkle.
3. Three matching catches, one per jump; a miss retries only that fly.
4. A true tongue-range catch from the lower-left platform using the normal final-catch zoom cinematic.
5. Wrong-fly spit-back.
6. Reachable mixed, two-match, and three-match multi-fly arcs.
7. Wall turn within two jumps; one jump is also accepted.
8. One-jump rebound catch.
9. Quick platform drop.
10. Buffered platform drop.
11. Double buffered drop with three catches.
12. Normal five-fly Park round and standard victory flow.

Scene transitions use a frog poof-out, an exact 500 ms delay, and a timed poof-in. The background continues running, existing flies stay still during the poof, and the frog’s previous pose remains visible until the transition begins. Tutorial activity is isolated from the legitimate cloud save. Completion awards **RIBBIT READY**.
