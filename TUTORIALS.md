# Ribbit Race Tutorials

## Single Player Tutorial
The first new profile/browser opens the guided Single Player tutorial once. It can be reopened from the book icon on the Single Player selection screen.

The sequence covers:
1. Terms and matching flies
2. Fully charged jumps
3. Automatic body/tongue catching range
4. Wrong-fly spit-back
5. Wall-only turning
6. Wall rebound catches
7. Quick platform drops
8. Buffered platform drops
9. Multi-fly jump arcs
10. A normal five-fly round

## Versus Tutorial
Open the book beside the Versus connection control.

The sequence covers:
1. Shared matching rules
2. Falling bonks
3. High/sky bonks
4. Stun immunity and recovery
5. Charge-dependent bounce strength
6. Platform blocking
7. Low-hop avoidance
8. Horizontal rebounds
9. Offensive rebound catches
10. Defensive interception
11. Rebound steals

The tutorial practice arena resets its setup when a required task is missed and does not affect cloud records.
