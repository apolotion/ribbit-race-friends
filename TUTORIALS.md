# Tutorial Overview — Ribbit Race Friends 1.7.7

The first-boot Single Player tutorial starts only after account authentication or guest selection and runs in the real Park engine.

## Presentation

- Ember speaks from a compact top dialogue panel with a right-side portrait.
- One sentence types at a time at 28 ms per character.
- Space/Enter or mobile tap-release reveals/advances text.
- Tutorial flies remain hidden until the last line closes, then use the normal spawn sparkle.
- Current Goal replaces the Time/Jumps/Best HUD.
- Pause is blocked during checked scenarios.

## Lessons

1. Welcome and objective.
2. Three consecutive 90%-plus charged jumps.
3. Three matching catches, one per jump.
4. Tongue-range catch with a brief slow-motion emphasis.
5. Wrong-fly spit-back.
6. Mixed, two-match, and three-match multi-fly arcs.
7. Wall turn within two jumps; one jump is also accepted.
8. One-jump rebound catch.
9. Quick platform drop.
10. Buffered platform drop.
11. Double buffered drop with three catches.
12. Normal five-fly Park round.

Failed authored attempts clear the tongue, poof out, wait 500 ms, restore the setup, and poof back in. Tutorial activity is isolated from the legitimate cloud save. Completion uses the normal Single Player victory flow and awards **RIBBIT READY**.
