RIBBIT RACE FRIENDS — PIN COMPATIBILITY NOTE
Build 2026.10.10-friends-centered-labels-themed-book-1

- New and returning players use username + a self-chosen four-digit PIN.
- The server stores a salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 derived verifier at 100,000 iterations, not the PIN itself.
- Existing PIN accounts and saves remain intact.
- Public old-private-key migration has been retired. The owner may use the guarded legacy-account recovery procedure when an obsolete key is permanently lost.
- Do not delete or recreate FriendsHub, FriendsRoom, or the Worker for this update.
