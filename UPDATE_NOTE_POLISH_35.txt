RIBBIT RACE FRIENDS — POLISH 35 / VERSION 1.7.23
Build: 2026.10.12-friends-polish-35

This release follows Polish 34 and implements the accumulated achievements, leaderboard, account, flash-card and online-menu changes.

1. Leaderboard medal: compact 28x28, gold laurel medal with red ribbons, centered in its 28x28 button. Ten pixel-art medal options are provided in public/icons/medal-options/ and also at the ZIP top level. A preview contact sheet is included. The default choice is public/icons/leaderboard-medal.png.
2. Clicking a leaderboard name opens a two-page Player Stats view, with the same metric cards and icons used in the normal Player Stats menu and a working top-right X.
3. Leaderboard Rank By and Level dropdowns use a smaller, single-color top header. Level choices use their own level icon.
4. Leaderboard replay cameras are derived from the deployed server's actual saved record_replays. New uploaded PB videos trigger a refresh. Cameras open saved record videos, and clicking names does not open replays. Existing historical PBs without a saved recording cannot be retroactively assigned a video.
5. Account & Language left-side button/hitbox moves right one pixel. Five menu options have distinct compact icons, color strips and the online-menu style.
6. Flash cards: animated card flip, frog secondary-color reverse, shuffled deck, persistent starred terms (S or /), English front whenever revisiting a card, concise centered controls with on-screen key labels, end-of-deck Restart/View Starred actions.
7. Hat unlock progress continues silently. Hat unlocks do not display menu or in-game toasts.
8. Solo/Versus tutorial book buttons reuse the pause-menu book icon sprite.
9. Frog Tags: opponent frog icon, last-played date, latest locally saved replay camera on the right, arrows at BACK button height, and a consistent BACK button at the same coordinates as the Online menu. The Quickplay BACK control now matches the Online menu button size and position.

Packaging:
- Full hosted project (Worker API, browser bundle, all source modules and tests).
- Polish 35 standalone offline HTML included for offline play.
- 10 transparent 28x28 medal PNGs + preview contact sheet.

Important deployment requirements:
- Deploy the COMPLETE Cloudflare Worker project, not just index.html or the public/ directory. Server-side stats and replay camera availability are provided by the Worker update.
- Cloud login, online matchmaking, online records and remote replay playback require a running hosted Friends deployment; the standalone HTML cannot provide those services by itself.
- Previously completed records without stored videos do not gain retroactive replays. Videos must be successfully captured/uploaded while the new Worker is live.
- No database migration is required: record_replays uses the already-existing table.
- Hosted/real-user production online integration was not tested from this environment.

Validation:
- Source compiles, 29 modules bundled, 23 assets present.
- 397/397 automated tests pass (including 9 Polish 35 regression tests).
- In-memory Chromium tested Account mouse entry, flash-card shortcuts and reverse color, clicked leaderboard player Stats grid and close X with mocked API data; zero browser JavaScript errors.
