Replay playback & user-interface improvements
- The first-jump “hold Space / release to jump” tutorial speech bubble is suppressed during both Solo and Versus tutorial play.
- Top-right in-game replay pause icon is replaced by a pink X inside the same box. The X exits replay playback.
- Clicking anywhere in gameplay while replay is playing pauses it. Interacting with replay toolbar remains separate.
- K toggles replay playback. Holding Space advances the replay temporarily at 2×; releasing restores its prior playback/paused state. Paused fast-forward stays silent. Playing fast-forward retains audio.
- Replay toolbar has left-aligned play/pause/replay symbol and a speaker button. Clicking the speaker toggles mute; hovering opens a draggable vertical volume slider.
- Up/Down arrow keys adjust replay volume in 5% steps. M toggles mute.
- Playback speed shows in a fixed-size pixel stopwatch with a transparent center and text ranging from 0.25× to 4×. Speed controls retain frame stepping and jumps.
- Replays end with LEVEL COMPLETE! rather than REPLAY COMPLETE!.
- Replay BEST values now refer to the worldwide leaderboard records for that level/category where available; unavailable data has an honest placeholder and never impersonates a player's own best.
- Replay countdowns from both new and older saved replay formats avoid cross-phase interpolation and reconstruct missing countdown frames with stable 60 FPS timing.
- Existing replay format and archive compatibility retained. No Cloudflare database migration required.
