VERSUS DEMONSTRATIONS
- Separated blocked player input from a frozen game simulation. "Watch Ember"
  demonstrations now run real frog movement, charges, collisions and catches.
- Flies animate during demonstrations and dialogue. Tutorial scenes no longer
  inject random normal-round flies into an authored lesson.
- Removed the old correction that repeatedly turned the player and pinned flies
  to fixed coordinates. Layout mirroring does not rewrite airborne movement.
- Both Space and Right Shift are blocked from disrupting scripted demo input.
- Demonstrations require actual outcomes, not timed success or distant fly
  collection. A missed demonstration resets and retries its authored scene.
- Re-tuned demonstrations exposed by the freeze fix: buffered sky drops,
  recovery/footstool, boost comparison, under bonks, low-profile movement,
  rebound catches and dinner-stealing.
- Scripted follow-up jumps preserve natural facing. Wall bounces and real
  opponent rebounds remain the ways to reverse direction in flight.
