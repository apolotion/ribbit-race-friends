PLAYING ON IPHONE?

Add Ribbit Race to your Home Screen for a larger, app-like play area.

  1. Open this game in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an upward arrow).
  3. Scroll and choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then launch Ribbit Race from the new icon.
  5. Rotate the iPhone sideways before starting a match.
