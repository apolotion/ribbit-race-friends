Friends Edition

One link. Your own save.

This game has its own cloud. It does not connect to the school’s Google Sheet, student database, or Google sign-in.

Start playing

Choose Create Account, pick a username, and choose your own 4-digit PIN. On another device, use that username and PIN to load your progress. Your current browser remembers the account with a session cookie for up to 30 days. Choose Play Without Saving for a temporary guest game.

Keep your PIN private

Your 4-digit PIN works like a password. Do not share it or reuse a sensitive PIN from another service. The server stores a salted derived hash rather than the PIN itself. We do not collect email addresses, so there is no emailed PIN reset.

Older private-key accounts

Public private-key migration has been retired. If an inaccessible legacy username is blocking a new account, contact the game owner for an owner-only recovery or deletion.

Play a friend

Open Versus → Wi-Fi. Choose Host and share the five-character room code, or choose Join and enter your friend’s code. Pick different frogs in a private room and both press Ready. Quickplay finds another person in this Friends Edition’s own queue. A queue with no other players will keep waiting.

Online guests are supported. Guest play does not earn a saved leaderboard record. Quickplay uses the game’s existing best-of-three, 60-second Endless rounds. Spectate and Battle Tags use this separate Friends server.

Controls and connection

Hold Space to charge, then release to jump in online play. Local two-player uses Left Shift and Right Shift. The SERVER … MS indicator is the round-trip to the other player through the room server, not a promise of zero delay. Keep the match tab open, the computer awake, and use a stable connection. The browser host runs the match simulation; Cloudflare transports live game messages.

Online matches cannot be manually paused. A reconnect or server-authorized CPU replacement can occur after connection loss. Browser sleep, page suspension, packet loss, geographic distance, and server limits can still interrupt play. A hard refresh ends the current in-memory game; create a new room afterward.

What this application stores

A saved account stores your username, an opaque account ID, a salted derived hash of your PIN, session hashes, progress, achievements, cosmetics, game records, private matchmaking ratings and recent matchup history. The application uses hashed network identifiers for short-lived rate limits. It does not intentionally store raw IP addresses in its account database or log private keys. Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, can process connection metadata under its own terms and settings.

Your username/display name and game records are visible to other players. Online rooms can appear in the spectator list, and spectators receive delayed recorded gameplay. Do not put a real name or private information in your username. “Private room” means code-based joining, not confidential gameplay. Guest sessions last up to six hours; temporary room, queue and replay records expire separately.

Manage or delete a save

Use Back Up / Manage Save from the account button in the game to export saved data, change a PIN, or delete the account. Changing the PIN or deleting requires the current PIN. Deleting removes the account’s saved progress and own profile/rating/history, but other players can retain historical matchup records. A backup is a copy of saved data, not a replacement sign-in key; automatic importing is not included.

Fair play and availability

This is a casual friends game. Local achievements and scores are client-reported, and the browser host runs multiplayer physics. The server authenticates room access and rejects duplicate result awards, but this is not a cheat-proof competitive service. Public signups and usage are limited to protect the installation. Ask the owner of your game link about availability, account limits, or abusive display names.
