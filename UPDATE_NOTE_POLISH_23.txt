RIGHT SHIFT / LOCAL VERSUS
- Removed the extra window-capture keyboard handler that sent Right Shift to
  P1 before the normal physical-input router also sent it to P2.
- Right Shift charges and releases only P2 in local two-player Versus. Left
  Shift remains P1. Releasing either key does not release the other frog.
- CPU and Versus-tutorial ownership are preserved: Right Shift does not take
  over Ember/the CPU and does not fall back to controlling P1.
- W remains a P1 alias, now in the normal held-key router instead of a second
  listener. W/Left Shift and Slash/Right Shift can overlap without a premature
  release. Single Player retains both Shift keys and W as jump controls.
- Existing online local-player ownership, mobile controls, and server protocol
  are unchanged; the duplicate P1 listener is removed from those screens too.

CATCH-CAMERA TITLES
- Contested fly zooms display CLOSE CATCH!, not FINAL CATCH!.
- FINAL CATCH! is reserved for reaching the configured score goal: e.g. the
  fifth fly in a five-fly level/round. A close contest on that goal-completing
  fly still correctly says FINAL CATCH!.
- The ten-fly option honors its actual ten-fly goal; Endless mode does not
  falsely treat the fifth fly as a level finish.
- Last-second/buzzer catches that do not reach a score goal use CLUTCH CATCH!;
  a close contest at that time still uses CLOSE CATCH!.
- The zoom, slow-motion physics, sounds and impact effects remain intact.
- A non-final cinematic does not consume the actual final-goal cinematic.
- Single Player no longer reads a stale Versus catch kind for its title.
- Authored tutorial practice cinematics use the existing title-suppression
  rule; the normal final five-fly tutorial round can still show FINAL CATCH!.
