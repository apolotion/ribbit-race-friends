FIX — FLY VOCABULARY TAGS DISAPPEARING IN VERSUS

CAUSE
- Several older compatibility layers permanently disabled fly word plates
  after ANY slow-motion critical-catch zoom, including non-winning contested
  catches. This could happen in the middle of a 60-second Versus round.
- After the cinematic ended, rendering of all surviving labels was blocked
  by global flags until another round/reset, so flies had no translations.

CHANGES
1. All still-visible fly terms return automatically after each slow-motion
   zoom; contested and clutch catches are no longer mistaken for permanent
   end-of-round suppression.
2. Fly terms retain their existing progressive fade-out during zoom and now
   fade back in over 0.42 seconds. Fade-in uses smooth easing, not a one-frame
   toggle, and leaves original vocabulary and word-plate placement intact.
3. A second catch started during fade-in cannot flash labels to full opacity
   before fading them away again.
4. Removed permanent hide gates from Solo, local Versus, Online spectator and
   shared rendering modules. Earlier final-catch compatibility hooks remain
   available but no longer suppress labels.
5. Fly-label fade state is saved per 60 FPS replay snapshot so newly recorded
   replays support smooth fade-in when seeking and frame-stepping. Older
   recordings derive a post-zoom fade from their recorded critical-catch frames.
6. Tutorial and round resets can restore labels immediately when opening a
   fresh scene; ordinary zooms use the gradual return fade.

  Frog Tags, replay controls and audio, tutorial chapters and cloud saves.
- No Code.gs update, Cloudflare Worker schema change, or D1 migration.
