VERSUS BONK STUN AND RECOVERY
- Ordinary bonks now produce a minimum 2.0-second stun after landing. The maximum sky-bonk stun remains 5.0 seconds.
- The tutorial continues its normal per-frog physics, stun countdown, landing squash, and single 0.56-second recovery rotation during Ember's dialogue and completed-lesson holds; no new player movement or scoring is allowed during these locks.
- A fresh second bonk resets the previous incomplete rotation; the victim still receives a complete rotation after the new stun expires. Skipping away from a lesson intentionally poofs to the next pose rather than delaying Space until recovery completes.

POOF, DIALOGUE, AND RETRY SAFETY
- Suppress invisible-frog airborne shadows, afterimages, tongue and jump previews during scripted poofs.
- Space input from a dialogue/poof cannot immediately charge a jump in the incoming scene; brief input guard and release lock are preserved across retries.
- Failed-attempt retries cannot retrigger a second poof during the same transition. Fresh rebound attempts clear old airborne and landing flags so a missed rebound causes one retry instead of a permanent repeating poof loop.

LESSON POSITIONS AND CHOREOGRAPHY

- Not So Fast practice now aims Ember's 86%-charged jump LEFT toward the middle-left platform, rather than turning right. The real built engine confirmed her landing on the middle-left platform.
