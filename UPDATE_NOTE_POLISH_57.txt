CHANGES
1) World Record hover: Results-screen World Record reward cards now have an
   interactive tooltip bound to their actual changing card positions.
   The hover is reevaluated every frame, so delayed award updates display
   the tooltip even if the mouse is stationary. It shows saved prior holder,
   score/time and record date when available; otherwise labels the leaderboard
   runner-up as NEXT BEST or says PREVIOUS HOLDER NOT RECORDED.
2) Replay countdown: Real initial 3-2-1 captured in new recordings; incomplete
   historic recordings receive a non-destructive, playback-only countdown.
3) Replay header: Original recording time and date, with stored recording
   timezone when available; inline font matches title (9:51 PM example).
4) Replay hotkeys: comma = back 1 frame, period = forward 1 frame.
   Shift-comma and Shift-period still adjust playback speed.
5) Zoom catch: Slow-motion fly remains drawn as it travels into the mouth
   along the visible tongue, including Solo and Versus and 60 FPS replays.
6) Toolbar: Icon play/pause/replay control, adjustable replay volume, fixed
   stopwatch playback-speed button (0.25x through 4x) on the right.
7) Result: REPLAY COMPLETE screen centered in the gameplay playfield,
   excluding the lower game HUD; score overlay does not freeze the dance.
8) Older replay v1 and v2 archives remain supported without modification.
