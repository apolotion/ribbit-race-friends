FIX 1 — VERSUS FLY DESPAWN WHEN RESUMING FROM PAUSE

CAUSE
- A legacy cross-screen countdown update deleted every visible fly and
  emptied game.spawnQueue each frame of any Versus countdown.
- That same countdown is used for unpausing a live round. In the absence of
  a fresh-round reset, resuming a pause permanently discarded both existing
  flies and scheduled replacement tickets.

CHANGES
- Removed countdown-time fly removal from the legacy update wrapper.
- Actual new-round and sudden-death transitions still intentionally reset
  flies via resetPvpGame() and beginPvpSuddenDeath().
- Both instant resume and optional 3-2-1 resume preserve fly objects, IDs,
  positions, word assignments, queued spawn tickets, and the spawn clock.
- No additional pause-screen UI is needed. Clock/spawn progression remains
  frozen while the game is paused or displaying the resume countdown.

FIX 2 — VERSUS FLY SPAWNING FEELS TOO SLOW

CAUSE
- Versus reused Solo's 2.0-second catch replacement delay.
- Worse, a shared Solo rule rescheduled all outstanding opening fly tickets
  after an early catch, spacing them another two seconds apart. That could
  postpone the Versus opening four-fly pool deep into an active round.

CHANGES
- Separate Versus replacement delay: 1.0 second (previously 2.0).
- Separate Versus minimum spawn spacing: 0.75 sec (previously 1.0).
- Initial Versus fly spacing: 0.85 sec (previously 1.05).
- A Versus catch no longer postpones existing opening spawn tickets.
- Four-fly replenishment and matching-target guards use the Versus timing.
- The sudden-death opener and authoritative Online Versus failover use the
  same Versus-specific cadence.
- Solo mode retains its original 2.0-second replacement, 2.0-second opening
  interval, and 1.0-second spawn spacing.
