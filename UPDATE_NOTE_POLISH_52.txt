REPLAY COUNTDOWN
- The recording starts on the first 3 of 3–2–1, before the tone is played.
- Online Versus synchronized countdown starts now seed the recorder at the first committed 3; the prior elapsed-time cutoff has been removed.
- New frame-based replays include 3, 2, 1, their tones, and game movement.
- Old recordings cannot retroactively include any countdown not recorded at the time.

REPLAY ENDING
- Frog victory hops alone for the first 1.0 second after the solo win.
- A centered, stepped REPLAY COMPLETE card shows final time/jumps/flies over the frog for the following 3.0 seconds.
- The frog continues hopping behind the score card, including the synthetic fourth second, until playback finishes.
- The score card is centered in both axes of the actual canvas rather than positioned near the top.
- Space OR the replay toolbar play button restarts finished playback from 0:00, including its first countdown tone.
- Normal pause/unpause behavior remains unchanged before the end of playback.

LEGACY PLAYBACK SUPPORT
- Explicit schema v2 for newly saved replays; archived v1 recordings remain supported through an in-memory adapter.
- The adapter accepts earliest Polish 44 frame records missing fps, duration, phase, audio, and win metadata.
- Polish 45–51 records remain watchable with their saved frames, skins, vocabulary, and sounds; old win timelines are adapted in memory without modifying stored data.
- Existing Frog Tags round/group recordings can be combined with newer set recordings.
- Original replay payloads remain unchanged in IndexedDB / cloud; replay viewing never deletes or rewrites them.
- New replay files embed a snapshot of the stage platforms and fly spawn points, protecting archived frog movement from future stage layout edits.
- Future replay updates should ADD new schema readers, never replace or remove v1/v2 readers. See REPLAY_COMPATIBILITY.md.
- Source-data-less historical video-only recordings cannot be reconstructed as game-state replays.

No API, Cloudflare Worker migration, or code.gs update is required.
