WHAT'S CHANGED

1. REPLAY COMPLETE / VICTORY HOP
- At the end of a completed Solo replay, the REPLAY COMPLETE! result card now overlays the active level instead of freezing the player on the last recorded frame.
- The normal victory-frog hopping/dancing animation continues for the full 2-second completion overlay hold. Scrubbing still freezes when paused; ordinary playback remains animated.
- Replaced the small rectangular completion card with a wider, stepped/pixel-outlined white result panel, a tall red shine-header, final time, jumps and flies, matching the shape and styling of the main FIVE FLIES end panel.
- FIND THE MATCH remains visible when the replay ends. Its English clue is blank during the completion hold.
- The 60 FPS recording system, countdown capture, audio, timeline shortcuts and Frog Tags set browser from Polish 47 are retained.

2. VERSUS TUTORIAL — WAIT FOR THE NEXT BONK
- Position the player (P1) at x=145 on the left ground, facing right, and Ember at x=305 on the right ground, facing left, consistent with the reference screenshot.
- Recalculate Ember's first real jump using the physical distance and the actual gravity/launch settings instead of a fixed charge calibrated for the old positions.
- Recalculate the return jump from Ember's actual post-bonk landing position. The jump aims toward the left wall; Ember changes direction ONLY when the real collision reverses her horizontal velocity, then rebounds back into P1 while stunned.
- Retain the buffered-platform-drop support and require a verified wall rebound before the two-bonk demonstration can be considered complete.

3. BONK-STUN RECOVERY ANIMATION
- Fixed recovery playing twice. The frog remains inverted throughout stun and plays ONE rotation back upright after the stun timer reaches zero. It no longer begins a rotation in the final moments of stun and then restarts it at the transition.
- Stop stale recovery rotation on a new bonk and clear it when a player is repositioned/reset.
- Clear the landing squash/pop state at the moment recovery begins, restoring a normal unsquashed frog once the rotation finishes.
