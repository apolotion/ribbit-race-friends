This is a COMPLETE GitHub/Cloudflare deployment ZIP based on Polish 46 (1.7.34).
It includes browser assets, Worker source, the game modules, and tests. Keep
existing Cloudflare data, player accounts, vocabulary, and game progress.
No database schema migration and no Apps Script code.gs changes are required.

REPLAY KEYS
- X / L: seek forward ten seconds; Z / J: seek backward ten seconds.
- Shift+N: next Frog Tags replay set; Shift+B: previous replay set.
- Existing 0–9 tenth seeking, frame stepping, speed changes, R toggle 4x,
  F fullscreen, Space pause, timeline scrubbing and audio are preserved.
- The in-game viewer has PREVIOUS, REPLAYS, and NEXT buttons for Frog Tags.
- Replacing one replay from another retains the correct original menu on Exit.

FROG TAGS
- The small replay icon on every opponent's row now opens a single scrollable
  menu of saved replay sets against that opponent; the menu can show all
  opponents and has a search filter.
- One NEW Frog Tag replay is recorded for each COMPLETE best-of match/set,
  instead of accidentally ending the recording after round one.
- The 60 FPS recorder continues across 3-2-1 countdowns, rounds, round-result
  celebrations and next-round transitions until the actual set winner exists.
- The archive stores the opponent, original recording date, win/loss, final
  score, and number of rounds.
- Older one-round recordings are explicitly labeled ROUND-ONLY; archives with
  several saved rounds from one match are assembled into one stitched set.
- All Frog Tag replays share the same in-engine replay experience, including
  flies and their vocabulary labels, tongue animation, synthesized sound,
  slow-motion catches, fullscreen, timeline and frame controls.
- The Frog Tags replay index retains up to 500 entries, deduplicating identical
  replay IDs. No old saved replay records or local progress are deleted.

REPLAY VIEWER
- The original replay date/time appears alongside the title (for example,
  FASTEST TIME or FEWEST JUMPS) when available; new recordings include a
  recordedAt timestamp. Older recordings without timestamps remain playable.
- At the replay's end, the FIND THE MATCH panel remains visible. Only the
  English clue below its title becomes blank.
- The original title and complete fly HUD remain inside the unobscured stage;
  the spectator toolbar/header live outside the stage.

LIMITATIONS
- The full high-frame-rate replay objects are saved to IndexedDB in the
  browser/device that recorded the match. Frog Tags summary metadata can be
  backed up with the normal game save, but an archived replay from a different
  device is not magically available as a video on this device. Missing older
  recordings are labeled unavailable; the menu never invents footage.
- Truly old video-only replay assets cannot be converted into native stage
  frames. Older frame-based round archives can still be watched/combined.
- Existing records can only contain what was actually recorded at that time;
  an older single-round clip cannot be reconstructed into an unwritten set.
